I love a good story.
The kind where you start somewhere, follow a few threads, take a wrong turn or two, and eventually end up somewhere you couldn't quite have predicted at the beginning. I suppose that's how I've ended up here too.

I've studied human anatomy, spent time buried in diagnostics and precision medicine, taught myself R to work with bioinformatics data, and now build apps and automations for a living. Somewhere along the way, I also decided to learn how to build for the web.

The common thread is probably that I like figuring things out. Give me a question, a problem, or something I don't quite understand yet, and I'm usually happy to follow it until things start making sense.

Outside of work, you'll probably find me reading, planning a trip I may or may not take anytime soon, watching something for far too long, or falling down another rabbit hole because I wanted to understand one small thing. This is my little corner of the internet for some of those things.
