About

I am drawn to the questions behind the work. What are we trying to do here, and how do we get there?

These questions have taken me in a few different directions.

I started out in the life sciences, where I became interested in the stories hidden in data. This led me to learn R and other technologies for bioinformatics to be able to answer the questions I was interested in.

That experience stuck with me, and often, I find the most interesting part of a problem is figuring out what you need to learn to solve it.

These days, I build applications and automations at Fingal County Council, working across Power Apps, Power Automate, Dataverse and other parts of the Microsoft ecosystem. I like taking something that starts as a messy process, an idea or a problem and working backwards until there's something useful at the other end.

I'm still figuring out exactly where that curiosity will take me, but the pattern has stayed pretty consistent: give me a problem worth understanding, and I'm happy to figure out the rest.
